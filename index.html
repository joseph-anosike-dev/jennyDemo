How Pre-Ordering Works

We make bulk importation simple by handling the factory communication, logistics, and shipping.

01

Choose an item

Browse our catalog and select the items you wish to pre-order. Submit your quantity and preferred shipping method.

02

Group order closes

Once we hit the factory's Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) across all customers, the pre-order window closes and manufacturing begins.

03

Goods leave China

Items are packed and handed over to our logistics partners for either Air or Sea shipping based on customer selection.

04

Arrival & collection

Goods arrive in Nigeria. Sea shipping customers must pay clearing/shipping fees within 3 days of arrival to avoid demurrage.

Shipping Methods

🚢 SEA SHIPPING

  • Duration: 2–3 months after goods leave China.
  • Cost: More economical. Best for bulky or heavy items.
  • Status: Our major shipping method.
  • Note: Maximum stated duration is 3 months, though it can arrive faster.

✈️ AIR SHIPPING

  • Duration: 7–14 days after goods leave China.
  • Cost: More expensive. Best for light, high-margin, or urgent items.
  • Status: Premium alternative.
  • Note: Calculated by actual or volumetric weight (whichever is higher).
Important Notice: Shipping durations and rates are subject to change based on global logistics, customs clearance, and airline/shipping line schedules. The business cannot guarantee exact arrival dates or fixed shipping rates.

Clear expectations protect everyone.

Please read our terms carefully. Importation involves inherent risks, and transparency is our core value.

⚠️

Pre-order nature: Items are NOT readily available in Nigeria. We are pooling resources to meet factory MOQs.

⏳

Payment windows: For sea shipping, payment becomes due immediately after arrival. You have exactly 3 days to pay.

💰

Demurrage: Failure to pay shipping within 3 days will result in daily demurrage charges added to your bill.

⚖️

Forfeiture: Goods unpaid for beyond our grace period may eventually be forfeited or auctioned to recover company costs.

🎨

Variations: We cannot guarantee exact product colors or sizes. Factory errors, though rare, are not guaranteed against.

📦

Transit Risks: Damage in transit is an inherent risk of importation. We pack carefully but cannot assume liability for courier mishandling.
